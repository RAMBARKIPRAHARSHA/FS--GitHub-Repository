Srikakulam (శ్రీకాకుళం)

"The Land of Temples & Pristine Coastline" — Sacred home to the world's most venerated Sun Temple at Arasavalli, the singular Kurma Avatar shrine, untouched beaches at Kalingapatnam, and the legendary handspun Ponduru Khadi.

Fast Facts

Reaching Srikakulam

Located on the northern coastal frontier along National Highway 16.

Nearest Airport

Visakhapatnam Airport (VTZ) is approximately 110 km south (approx. 2 hours scenic drive via NH-16 expressway).

Railway Station

Srikakulam Road (CHE) is located at Amadalavalasa (12 km from city center). Frequent autos and local buses link the station to the city.

National Highway

NH-16 seamlessly connects Srikakulam with Visakhapatnam (110 km), Vizianagaram (75 km), and Bhubaneswar (330 km).

Ratha Saptami

Celebrated in January/February at Arasavalli. Over 300,000 pilgrims assemble to welcome Lord Surya's solar chariot with sacred offerings.

Astronomical Feat

The Bi-Annual Solar Ray Wonder

Constructed in the 7th century CE by Kalinga monarch Devendra Varma, the Arasavalli Sun Temple is engineered with astounding astronomical precision. Twice each year—during Uttarayana (March) and Dakshinayana (October)—the early morning rays of the sun penetrate directly through all five consecutive entrance gateways to fall squarely on the feet of Lord Surya in the inner sanctum.

  • Five Gateway Alignment: Precision ancient Kalinga architectural engineering.
  • Indra Pushkarini: Sacred bathing tank whose waters are believed to heal ailments.
  • Chariot with 7 Horses: Intricately sculpted idol of Surya flanked by Usha and Chhaya.

Living GI Craft

Ponduru: Gandhi’s Beloved Khadi

Ponduru is globally celebrated as the only craft village where indigenous short-staple hill cotton is combed using the jawbone of the Valuga fish. The fine teeth of the fish jaw remove seed impurities without breaking delicate fibers, producing fabric so whisper-light that Mahatma Gandhi declared it India’s finest.

Empowering 1,000+ Women Artisans Visit the Andhra Fine Khadi Karmik Sangham (AFKK) in Ponduru to witness live spinning and purchase authentic GI-tagged cotton sarees.
