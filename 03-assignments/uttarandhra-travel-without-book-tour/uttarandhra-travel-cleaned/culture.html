Culinary Wonders

Flavors of North Coastal Andhra

Six signature culinary icons you simply cannot leave Uttarandhra without tasting.

Araku Valley / Visakhapatnam

Araku Bongu Chicken (Bamboo Chicken)

Tender country chicken marinated in local ginger, garlic, wild cumin, and Eastern Ghats forest herbs. Stuffed into fresh green bamboo stems, sealed with sal leaves, and roasted slowly over glowing charcoal with zero added cooking oil.

Must Try: Araku Valley hill shacks, Rushikonda Tribal Dhabas
All 3 Coastal Districts

Coastal Chepala Pulusu (Fish Curry)

Fresh catch from the Bay of Bengal or River Vamsadhara cooked in an authentic earthenware pot. The tangy, fiery red gravy is made from aged tamarind, crushed mustard, fenugreek, and spicy green chillies. Tastes even richer on day two!

Must Try: Sea Inn (Raju Gaari Dhaba) at Rushikonda Beach
Visakhapatnam (Madugula)

Authentic Madugula Halwa

Born in 1890 by confectioner Dangeti Dharma Rao, this legendary halwa is made by soaking whole wheat, extracting rich wheat milk, and slow-cooking it for hours in pure desi ghee, organic sugar, cashews, and almonds. Melts luxuriously like silk.

Must Try: Dangeti Murthy Halwa shop in Madugula / Vizag
Visakhapatnam Beach Road

RK Beach Muri Mixture & Tomato Bajji

The quintessence of Vizag's seaside culture. Crispy puffed rice mixed fresh with crunchy roasted peanuts, finely diced red onions, tangy raw mango, spiced boiled potatoes, and fresh lime juice, paired with piping hot deep-fried tomato cutlets.

Must Try: Stalls opposite Kursura Submarine (from 5 PM)
Vizianagaram / Bobbili

Bobbili Bellam Jilebi & Ariselu

Unlike traditional sugar-syrup jalebis, Bobbili's specialty spirals are dipped into hot organic golden sugarcane jaggery (Bellam) syrup brewed from local farms, granting them a deep caramel crust and rich unrefined sweetness.

Must Try: Bobbili Royal Bazaar sweet confectioneries
Srikakulam River Country

Srikakulam Chitti Garelu & Nattu Kodi

Bite-sized, ultra-crispy black-gram lentil fritters (Garelu) paired with a piping bowl of spicy country rooster curry (Nattu Kodi Pulusu) or freshly ground ginger-tamarind Allam Pachadi. The celebratory morning feast of Srikakulam.

Must Try: Nagavali riverfront breakfast messes in Srikakulam
Master Craftsmanship

GI-Tagged Handicrafts & Living Arts

Heritage traditions preserved across centuries by master artisan guilds.

GI Tagged Musical Instrument

The Bobbili Saraswati Veena

Carved from a single solid log of seasoned Jackfruit wood without joints (Ekanda Veena). The Sarvasiddhi artisans in Gollapalli hand-carve intricate lion head finials and inlay lion pearls, generating an incomparable pure acoustic sustain.

Where to buy: Bobbili Veena Society Showroom, Gollapalli Colony
GI Tagged Textile Wonder

Ponduru Fish-Bone Khadi

Indigenous red hill cotton is gently combed using the dried jawbone of the freshwater Valuga fish. The naturally fine teeth remove cotton impurities without snapping delicate short staple fibers, hand-spun up to 100 count counts.

Where to buy: AFKK Khadi Karmik Sangham, Ponduru Town
Cultural Extravaganza

Spectacular Regional Festivals

Plan your visit around these spellbinding religious and folk celebrations.

Vizianagaram Sirimanotsavam

Annual (First Tuesday After Dussehra)

A colossal folk festival where the chief priest is strapped atop a 60-foot swaying neem wood pole (Sirimanu), riding through the royal Gajapati Fort streets to bless hundreds of thousands of gathered pilgrims.

Simhachalam Chandanotsavam

Akshaya Tritiya (April / May)

The only day in the entire 365-day year when the thick sandalwood paste encasing Lord Varaha Narasimha is removed, revealing the Nijaroopam (original deity form) to over 150,000 devotees from across the nation.

Arasavalli Ratha Saptami

Surya Jayanti (January / February)

The celestial birthday of the Sun God. Over 300,000 pilgrims converge on Srikakulam to take holy dips in the Indra Pushkarini tank and offer payasam cooked in traditional clay pots over open cow-dung fuel.
